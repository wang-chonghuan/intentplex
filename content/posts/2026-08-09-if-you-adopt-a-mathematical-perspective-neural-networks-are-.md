If you adopt a mathematical perspective, neural networks are nothing more than manifold mappings and optimization in high-dimensional space.

Whether word vectors or latent spaces, the core way AI processes data is by mapping real-world concepts into high-dimensional continuous spaces.  
Gradient descent is not merely something in the code—it is the descent of potential energy on a high-dimensional non-convex surface. Where are the minima of the loss function? Does the manifold collapse? Once you master measure theory and differential geometry, you will understand why models exhibit remarkable generalization ability along certain dimensions.
