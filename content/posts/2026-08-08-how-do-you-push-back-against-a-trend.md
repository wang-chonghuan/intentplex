How do you push back against a trend?  
Sometimes the smartest move is to fully lean into it first.

Only after I’ve actually let AI run unattended for ten hours and deliver a genuinely useful, polished app will I feel I’ve earned the right to say things like “bring the human back into the loop.”

Until then, people will just say I’m not AI-native enough.
