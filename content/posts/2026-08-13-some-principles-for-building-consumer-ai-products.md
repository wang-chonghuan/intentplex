Some principles for building consumer AI products:

1. Keep it small. Focus on solving one clear problem. Skip the big vision.

2. Start from your own real needs. Only build things that solve problems you’ve actually experienced in daily life or work.

3. It’s fine if similar products already exist — but yours must have one distinct angle that makes it meaningfully different.

4. If the product involves AI, it must support BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) or integrate a local agent. No forced dependency on a single provider.

5. Don’t make free products. Keep the price low, pricing transparent, and keep shipping updates.

6. No community groups. Handle communication through email + issues. Continuously improve the documentation and make it AI-friendly.

7. Offer easy refunds. Don’t waste time and energy on low-quality customers — just refund them and move on. Your time and mental health matter more.
