The core of harness engineering is not building smarter individual skills, but validating and handing off the artifacts between them.

Smart coding or review skills alone are not enough. Without a closed loop for validation and handoff, they introduce noise, accumulate anomalies, and eventually destabilize the whole development process.

A reasonably smart model with ordinary skills, running inside a disciplined loop, can outperform smarter models or even skills written by star developers when those skills fail to form a closed loop.
