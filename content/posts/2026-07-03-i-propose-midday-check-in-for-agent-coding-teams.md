I propose Midday Check-in for agent coding teams. Daily standup should review yesterday’s outcomes and align today’s plan. Midday check-in is not about pushing progress, but about preventing deviation. When AI greatly increases output speed, a wrong direction can quickly turn into hours of bad code.
