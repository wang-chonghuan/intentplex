Imagine every worker ant in a colony suddenly became 4x more productive.

Soon, they’d start hauling in everything they could find, building whatever they wanted, and ignoring the original design.

The colony would quickly be DDoS’d by redundant rooms, bad materials, and endless expansion.

Eventually, the ants would have to stop building and start repairing a nest collapsing under its own weight.

That’s what AI is doing to software development teams in 2026.
