I’m not optimistic about #Eve, #Vercel’s newly released agent building framework. What people lack today are agent skills that execute strict workflows, not agents that can freely decide which tools to use. Skills are what enable work to be completed strictly according to human intent. The enormous degree of freedom that agents have means they can only be used for exploration.
