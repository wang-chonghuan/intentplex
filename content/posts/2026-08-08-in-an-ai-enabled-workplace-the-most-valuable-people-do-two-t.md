In an AI-enabled workplace, the most valuable people do two things well: they use AI to develop the best approach, and they apply strong judgment to review, challenge, and improve its output.
If someone still relies on others to define the approach, while not being rigorous enough in reviewing AI-generated work, that is an area where they need to grow.
