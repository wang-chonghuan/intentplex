If your team isn’t building a content farm or an app farm, don’t chase highly automated, unattended development. Automation is a natural byproduct of mature processes—not something you can force by treating automation itself as the goal and expecting your team’s processes to magically mature as a result. Just look at #OpenClaw’s sharp drop in traffic: you’ll see that the hype around spinning up dozens of agents to develop in parallel simply doesn’t work. It’s mostly commercial marketing.
