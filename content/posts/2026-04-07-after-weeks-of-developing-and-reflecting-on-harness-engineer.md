After weeks of developing and reflecting on harness engineering, I have finally locked in a definitive direction: humans will focus exclusively on defining requirements, discussing technical solutions, and validating results.
Every other stage of the lifecycle—including development, testing, deployment, operations, and documentation—will be handled entirely by AI. These processes will become transparent (invisible) to the human team.
