AI hasn’t reduced programming jobs — it has increased them. Human desire is unlimited; we constantly want things faster, better, and more abundant. If someone thinks AI-driven productivity means we need fewer programmers, they may be underestimating how many new opportunities and possibilities greater leverage can create.
