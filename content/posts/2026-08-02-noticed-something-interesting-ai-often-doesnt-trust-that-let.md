Noticed something interesting: AI often doesn't trust that letting itself work autonomously gives better results. It always wants to write rigid, mechanical code to handle a problem, rather than spinning up a subagent and directing it to explore with a simple prompt. So the thing holding AI back from its full potential is quite possibly AI itself.
