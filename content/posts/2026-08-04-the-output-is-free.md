The output is free. The cost is paid by those who review it.
That’s my biggest takeaway from the flood of AI-generated code.

Advice for developers:
1. Read requirements line by line — AI can rephrase, but you must still read them yourself. 
2. Align on a clear plan with AI first. Fully understand it before coding. 
3. Personally verify results against acceptance criteria, one by one. 

Advice for requirement owners:
1. Write the requirements yourself, or review every line carefully. 
2. Never just pass an AI-generated document to developers.
