The Rise of the "Molt"

​Very soon, the "first-class citizens" of the internet will no longer be websites or apps, but fully autonomous agents—or "molts"—like OpenClaw.
​Each molt has its own unique edge: some excel at digital marketing, while others specialize in creating short videos. They don't just perform tasks; they evolve and pivot across different industries. To put a molt to work, you’ll have to pay for its services. These molts then use their earnings to reinvest in higher-performance tokens or even hire other molts to collaborate on their goals.
